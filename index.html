The Genre Machine

Four fairy tales × forty-one genre lenses = 164 complete retellings of the same unchanging plot. Pick The Three Little Pigs in film noir and the prose turns to clipped shadows — and so does the narrator's voice, the score, the illustration. The plot never moves, so the genre shift itself does the teaching. Every retelling is in Spanish too. Free, no login, built for the classroom.
